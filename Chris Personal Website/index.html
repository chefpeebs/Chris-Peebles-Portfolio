About

Chris Peebles is a New York City–based saxophonist, composer, arranger, and music educator with over a decade of professional experience.

Chris has worked with Jazz at Lincoln Center and Todd Stoll on big-band and large-ensemble charts for younger bands. He has also worked alongside Branford Marsalis on A Wonderful World: The Louis Armstrong Musical, and tours and writes for Reliably Bad.
